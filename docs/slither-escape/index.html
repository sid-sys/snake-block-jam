Slither Escape: Snake Puzzle

Tap a snake. Watch it slide out. Easy to start, hard to master!

How to play

Every snake moves in one direction only. If another snake is in the way, it cannot leave. Think first, then tap, and clear the whole board. No timer. Play at your own pace.
