Privacy Policy for Slither Escape

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Slither Escape ("the game") is published by Core Interactive ("we", "us"). This page explains what information the game uses.

What we do not collect

The game has no account and no sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. We do not run our own servers that store your personal data.

What is stored on your device

Your progress (level, coins, lives, settings, purchases you made) is saved only on your device. If you delete the game, this data is removed.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads using Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising identifier, your approximate location (taken from your internet address), and information about how you interact with ads and the app, to show ads and measure them. Your device settings let you limit this: on Android use Settings > Privacy > Ads, on iPhone use Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. More information: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

In-app purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see or store your payment card details.

Diagnostics

The game and its ad and purchase services may collect crash reports and performance data to find and fix problems.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
